Progression

Levels, skills, gear and clubs

Three things gate a club: your level, your skills and the kit you wear. Level comes from XP. Skills come from the seven formula families at the Nets. Kit comes from coins, spent at Maya's Store. Every number here is data the server sends, so you can tune it without touching the world.

1 PlayTalk to coaches, workers and wanderers. Each visit is a new question. Right answers pay coins, XP and skill XP.
2 PrepareOpen Career to see what is missing. Buy and wear the required kit at Maya's. Raise the listed skills.
3 EnrolVisit the club recruiter. When every line is ticked, start the selection trial. The server enrols you and pays a bonus.

Side quests

Selector Sana. The notice board in Town Square hands out quests across every skill. Workers. The tea stall, kit repair and pavilion each ask about real jobs they do. Wanderers. Six people walk the Maidan, the park and the club road. Catch them and talk. Timed matches. Shield Cup every 6 hours, Inter Divisions every 12, the server owns the clock. Relaxed timing doubles the seconds.

Pacing, as the mock plays it

A question pays roughly 18 to 36 coins and 30 to 50 XP, rising with difficulty. Level 2 needs about 3 answers. Level 8 needs about 60. The cheapest upgraded kit costs 50 coins. A full upgrade of all four slots is about 300 coins. The server owns all of these numbers. The mock only imitates them.
