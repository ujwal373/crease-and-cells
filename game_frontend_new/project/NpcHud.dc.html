The HUD, on a phone

Everything sits on dark plates, so it reads over grass, sand and brick alike. The middle of the screen is left free for the world.

1Level and XP. A gold LV badge, the player name, a bar and the number, for example 60 / 100 XP. The bar is never the only signal. 2Coins. Coin icon and a big number. The number pops when it changes, then settles. 3Zone name. A pill that always spells the zone out. Gold for The Nets, teal for The Club House, with the name in words. 4Joystick. 136 px, bottom left, touch only. Desktop shows key hints instead. 5Map and Career buttons. Map (key M) and Career (key C) stack above Customise in the live prototype. The Store is no longer a button: it is Maya, standing in Town Square. 6Customise button. Shirt icon and the word Customise. Lists what you own and lets you change what you wear. Buying happens at Maya’s Store. 7Talk prompt. Appears only when a character is in range. Shows the E key on a keyboard, and works as a plain button on touch.
Rules for every HUD element Text is at least 15 px. Contrast is at least 4.5 to 1. Targets are at least 48 px. Every icon has a word beside it. Messages are announced to screen readers, and sit on screen for four seconds.
