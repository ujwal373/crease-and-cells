Constraints, answered

Light, licensed, fair and open to everyone

The five rules from the brief, turned into things a builder can check.

1 The server decides, the world displays

CallWhat the server decidesWhat the world does
GET /api/worldLevel, XP, skills, owned gear, and a marker for every character.Draws the markers and the HUD. Never works them out itself.
POST /api/npcs/:id/encounterThe question, store view or recruiter view. Written fresh by that character's agent.Slides the panel over the world. No scene change.
POST /api/encounters/:id/answerWhether the answer is right, the coins, XP and skill paid, and any level up.Shows the result and replaces its player copy.
POST /api/encounters/:id/trialWhether the player may join a club, re-checked on the server.Shows the welcome message.
POST /api/store/buy and /api/avatar/equipAffordability, ownership and the saved look.Swaps the matching slot on the hero.

In the prototype a stand in plays the server and the agents, with a short delay. Full request and response shapes are on the Contract board.

2 Runs on a mid range phone

BudgetLimit
EngineThree.js, no heavy framework.
Visible triangles25,000 or fewer in view.
Draw calls60 or fewer. Trees, palms and mesh fences are instanced.
LightsOne directional and one hemisphere. Nothing else.
ShadowsBaked ground planes and blob shadows. No real time shadow maps.
TexturesVertex colour. One 512 px atlas for signs and mesh.
Each model60 KB or less, compressed with meshopt.
First load1.5 MB or less. Only The Nets and the hero.
Lazy loadingDistricts stream in as the camera nears them. The full world is 2400 by 1400 units and only what is within about 1000 units of the camera is drawn.
ResolutionPixel ratio capped at 1.5. Frame rate capped at 30 on low power.
HeroAbout 900 triangles. Each slot is its own small mesh, swapped on purchase.

3 Art that is ours or CC0

Every model is either generated in code or taken from a CC0 pack. Nothing else goes in the repo. No real players, teams, clubs or brands: character names are invented, kits have no logos, and signs use plain words.

Every asset gets one row in assets/LICENCES.csv with its file, source, author, licence, link and date added. A build check fails if a model has no row.

4 Open to everyone

Readable Body text is at least 16 px, HUD text at least 15. Contrast is at least 4.5 to 1. The page still works at 200 percent zoom.
Not colour only Markers differ by shape and symbol. Selected items get a tick and a thick border. Results show an icon and a word as well as a colour. Colours that must differ also differ in lightness.
Keyboard friendly Walk, talk, close, shop and answer without a mouse. A thick gold focus ring shows where you are. Tab reaches every character and button.
Screen readers The mission panel is a dialog. Focus moves into it, then back to the world on close. Rewards and level ups are announced politely.
Calm motion With reduced motion on, the camera snaps, markers stop bobbing and panels appear without sliding.
No pressure No timers on missions. A wrong answer costs nothing, and a hint is always free.
