Camera and controls

Third person, three quarter view

A fixed angle camera follows the player from behind and above. It never rotates, so the same controls work on a keyboard and on a thumb, and nobody gets lost.

Seen from above. The camera stands south east of the player and looks north west, a turn of 45 degrees. That is why the south and east faces of buildings are the ones you see, and why screen up means north west.

Keyboard

W ASD
or
↑ ←↓→
ETalk. Opens the mission panel for the nearest character. Enter and Space also work. M CMap and Career. M opens the full map where you pick a place to walk to. C opens your skills and club ladder. EscClose. Slides the panel away and returns you to the world. TabJump. Moves between nearby characters and the Map, Career and Customise buttons. Enter on a character walks you there.

Every action in the game can be done from the keyboard alone.

Phone

Joystick, bottom left. Under the left thumb. It is 136 px across, so it fits a small hand and never hides the player. Buttons, bottom right. Map, Career and Customise, each with an icon and a word. Talk button. Appears above the joystick when a character is close. Tap a character. The hero walks over and opens the mission.
