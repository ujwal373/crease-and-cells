Map layout

The whole world, 5600 by 5600, a square

The Nets and Town Square are home, in the south west. A north south avenue climbs from there, and each division has its own venue off it, in order, ending at the huge National stadium in the north east. Grounds are round, and the lawns between them are left open to roam. Gold dashed boxes are facilities that pay points when you stand in them.

Venues, in order

University of Mumbai. Big university building with a tower and library. The round university maidan sits beside it behind a hedge, with a members gate on its south side. Entrance exam: 3 questions, 2 right. Daily practice on the maidan once in.Shield Cup. Round ground with a horseshoe of stands, just east of the university. Selector Kamat. Timed match: 5 questions, 90 seconds each, 3 right. Once every 6 hours.Borivali District Club. Heritage clubhouse and bar beside a well kept ground. Gate steward, club secretary, then Arjun: 3 checkpoints.Inter Divisions. Mishra on the same ground. Timed match: 5 questions, 90 seconds each, 3 right. Once every 12 hours.State Trophy ground. Big round ground with stands, three trophy cabinets, a large club building and a prepared pitch. Lad sir runs the entrance exam.Harbour Blues town. A small town with two streets: HQ, shop, cafe, hotel, gym, aqua centre, sauna, nets with a bowling machine, and two big round grounds to the south. Walking in a facility earns points with no questions. Three coaches teach on request. Two head coaches run match days on Ground A and B.National XI. A huge double tier stadium with floodlight masts and a flag on a tall mast, plus an academy with its own gym and pool. Three checkpoints, the captain exam, then the coach runs the stadium match for large rewards.

Reading the map

CCoach, gold circle. Seven lanes at the Nets. RRecruiter, outlined diamond. Entrance exam. M KTimed match or checkpoint, gold square. L PLesson coach or daily practice, gold circle. F TFriend (dashed) or staff (plain), outlined circle. S N WStore, quest master, works here.
Shape and letter carry the meaning, never colour alone. Pale roads and dashed lines are the paths Auto walk follows.
