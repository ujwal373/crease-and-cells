How it is built to look

1 Flat shaded low poly Every colour has a light, a base and a shade tone. Faces are flat. There are no photo textures, only vertex colour and one small atlas for signs and net mesh.
2 Long violet shadows Shadows are baked planes and blob shadows tinted Shadow Violet at about 28 percent. They fall to the east and south, away from the sun.
3 One sun, one fill One directional light and one hemisphere light per zone. A zone changes mood by changing their colours, never by adding lights.
4 Readable at 360 pixels Chunky shapes, thick paths, big characters. Anything you can interact with carries a shape, a label and a colour.
From your first reference The painterly street scene gives us the building colours (salmon walls, teal glass, slate roofs), the shadow shapes and the way hedges and trees overlap the ground. We keep the muted warmth and drop the grime.
From your second reference The bright toy city gives us rounded kerbs, chunky trees, clear paving rhythm and landmarks you can spot from far away. We keep the readability and drop the saturation a notch. Nothing is copied from either.
